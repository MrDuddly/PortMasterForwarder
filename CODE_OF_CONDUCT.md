# Code of Conduct

PortMasterForwarder is a friendly place for people who like organizing their retro handhelds.

## Our standards
- Be respectful and patient; assume good intent.
- Welcome newcomers and people with different levels of experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Keep discussions on topic. No harassment, discrimination, insults, personal attacks or publishing other people's private information.
- Do not ask for or share pirated games or copyrighted game files in issues, discussions or pull requests.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that do not follow this code, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.
To report a problem, contact the maintainers through the contact information on the repository owner's GitHub profile.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
